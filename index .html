FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with FameZone.

How does Instagram growth work with FameZone? +
FameZone promotes your Instagram profile and content through targeted marketing campaigns. We identify users who are genuinely interested in your niche — based on their interests, behaviors, and engagement patterns — and place your content in front of them through our advertising and influencer partner network. This drives real, organic discovery of your account by the right people.
Are the followers real users? +
Absolutely. FameZone works exclusively through targeted content promotion and marketing — not artificial methods of any kind. Every person who follows you does so because they discovered your content through our promotional campaigns and chose to follow you. We never use bots, automation, or inauthentic engagement methods. Your growth is 100% real.
Do I need to share my password? +
No, never. FameZone does not require your Instagram password or login credentials at any point. Our service works entirely through external marketing promotion. We only need your public Instagram username and some details about your niche and target audience. Your account security and privacy are always fully protected.
When will I start seeing results? +
Most clients start seeing increased profile visits, engagement, and follower growth within the first 7–14 days of their campaign going live. Significant, sustained growth typically becomes clearly visible within 30 days. Results vary based on your niche, content quality, and chosen plan. Our team will guide you in setting realistic expectations during your onboarding session.
